# Future STS2 evidence bridge

The archived bridge may later capture versioned snapshots, legal actions, RNG
vectors, and guarded transition traces as parity evidence. Reactivation
requires an accepted replacement specification, reviewed game-version
fingerprinting, localhost-only transport, explicit live-mutation consent, no
secret collection, and isolated compatibility tests. Version 0.3 does not
compile, install, or execute the bridge.
